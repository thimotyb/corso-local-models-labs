# Figure attribution

The six chapter 2 figures in `assets/` and embedded in `02_bert_text_classification.ipynb` come from the [Natural Language Processing with Transformers notebooks](https://github.com/nlp-with-transformers/notebooks/tree/main/images). They are redistributed under the repository's Apache License 2.0, copied in `assets/UPSTREAM-LICENSE`. The figures are used with new captions and in a newly written, updated course exercise. The social-media example figure carries the original source credit to Aditya Veluri.

The notebook follows the teaching sequence of the original text-classification chapter while rewriting the explanations and code for the present lab. The [DAIR.AI emotion dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dair-ai/emotion) is downloaded when the notebook runs; its dataset card states educational and research use.
